HERMANN PARK PAVILION CONCEPT — DP2

START HERE
Open Pavilion-Walkthrough.html in Chrome, Safari, or another WebGL-capable desktop browser. The viewer is self-contained and works without an internet connection. Drag to rotate the view, pinch or scroll to zoom, and select the five viewpoint buttons for different parts of the concept.

FILES
Pavilion-Walkthrough.html — complete interactive viewer.
Pavilion-Concept.glb — editable 3D asset. In Blender, use File > Import > glTF 2.0. Units represent meters.
scene.js — procedural model source.
main.js — viewer and panorama-export source.
index.template.html — viewer interface before bundling.
vendor/ — bundled Three.js 0.180.0 files, MIT-licensed.
export.mjs — Node exporter for the GLB file.

PHONE VIEWING
To use a phone browser, put Pavilion-Walkthrough.html on an HTTPS host and open its URL. Touch viewing does not require a headset. The Phone look-around button requests motion access where supported. A local file or an ordinary HTTP address may not permit phone motion. Browser and operating system support vary.

CARDBOARD / HEADSET VIEWING
Export 360 photo creates a 2:1 equirectangular PNG from your current location. Import that image into a phone app that explicitly supports equirectangular photos and your Cardboard-style viewer. This gives a fixed-position look-around experience. It does not provide movement through the building or binocular depth. Check the selected app with your phone and viewer before buying hardware or relying on it for testing.

Enter headset VR appears only when the browser reports immersive WebXR headset support. It is intended for a compatible headset/browser. The viewer does not implement calibrated Cardboard optics.

VIEWER TOOLS
Save this view exports the current 3D view as a PNG.
Export 360 photo exports a panorama for a separate 360 viewer.
Phone look-around uses motion sensors where available.

DESIGN CHOICES
This first concept is on land beside an illustrative lake, with a terrace toward the water. It includes a glass enclosure, an interior weatherproof roof, a larger louvered shade canopy, indoor study tables, outdoor dining, public benches, a food counter, a water refill station, a service/restroom block, and optional feeder props. Room layouts and people are schematic. The site is not surveyed and the furniture/structure are approximate. The photoreal concept image is a separate illustration and is not an exact rendering of this mesh.

CHECKED
The desktop browser displayed the model, switched to the study and lake viewpoints, and exported a 3072 x 1536 panorama. The GLB exported successfully. Physical phone motion, phone comfort, and headset sessions have not been tested.

SUGGESTED RESEARCH FOCUS
Test whether shaded seating near the water supports staying longer, including for visitors who do not purchase food. Ask participants to choose a place to rest, locate water or a restroom, or choose a study place. Watch their choices and ask about specific moments. Food pricing, no-purchase seating rules, sounds, and thermal comfort require additional props or role-play alongside the model.
